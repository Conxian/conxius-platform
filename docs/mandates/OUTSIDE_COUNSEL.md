# Conxian-Labs: Outside Counsel Criteria

This document defines the criteria for engaging outside counsel at Conxian-Labs, as per [CON-280].

## Required Capabilities
- **Startup Law**: Experience with entity formation, equity structures, and venture financing.
- **Commercial Contracts**: Ability to draft/review SDK licensing agreements, partnership MOUs, and vendor contracts.
- **Intellectual Property**: Strategy for protecting Conxian's "Real Rails" and sovereign primitives.
- **Regulatory/Fintech**: Deep expertise in digital asset regulation, stablecoin compliance (specifically ZAR/sBTC), and privacy law.
- **Autonomous Systems**: Familiarity with legal implications of AI-driven financial orchestration and agentic custody.

## Near-Term Launch Decisions
- Review of ALEX-linked liquidity pool terms.
- Compliance verification for the Universal Bitcoin Identity (UBI) protocol.
- Legal structuring of the SAB/DAO handoff.

## Selection Process
- Candidates must provide a track record of working with Bitcoin-native or Stacks-based projects.
- Preferred experience with "Sovereign Tech" ethos.
- Transparent billing and "agent-friendly" collaboration tools.
