# Conxian-Labs: Wave 1 Hiring Mandates

This document defines the mandates, outcomes, and success criteria for Wave 1 hires at Conxian-Labs, as per [CON-278].

## 1. Engineering Lead
**Mandate**: Lead the technical delivery of the Conxian Gateway and Nexus Glass Node, ensuring production-grade reliability and security.
**Reporting Line**: Founder
**Success Outcomes**:
- Successful Mainnet deployment of Gateway v1.0.
- Zero critical vulnerabilities in on-chain settlement logic.
- 99.9% uptime for Nexus state synchronization.
**Hiring Criteria**: Deep Rust/Clarity expertise, distributed systems experience, track record of launching protocol-level infrastructure.

## 2. Security & Infrastructure Lead
**Mandate**: Guard the Conxian perimeter, manage institutional secrets, and orchestrate sovereign node distribution.
**Reporting Line**: Founder
**Success Outcomes**:
- FIPS 140-2 Level 3 HSM integration complete.
- Automated secret rotation and audit logging active.
- Multi-cloud (GCP/Akash) redundancy verified.
**Hiring Criteria**: DevSecOps background, HSM/TEE expertise, experience with sovereign infrastructure (Akash, Kwil).

## 3. Product & Execution Lead
**Mandate**: Translate the Conxian ethos into market-ready financial products and manage community/bounty execution.
**Reporting Line**: Founder
**Success Outcomes**:
- Launch of ALEX-linked liquidity pools.
- Completion of Phase 6 "Real Rails" roadmap.
- High community engagement and bounty delivery quality.
**Hiring Criteria**: Fintech/DeFi product experience, strong execution focus, ability to bridge strategic vision and technical implementation.

## Onboarding Expectations
- All hires must complete a "Sovereign Audit" of their respective domains within 30 days.
- Weekly alignment with the Office of the Founder.
- Mandatory participation in the OpenSpec review process.
