# Research: Bitcoin-Standard Business Operations (2026)

## 1. Market Realities
- **Institutional Adoption**: Public companies (e.g., MicroStrategy, Tesla) holding BTC as a primary reserve asset.
- **Operational Bitcoin**: SMEs in Global South (El Salvador, Nigeria) using BTC/Lightning for day-to-day settlements to avoid local currency inflation.
- **Layer 2 Maturity**: Stacks (sBTC) and Lightning are the primary rails for fast, low-cost business transactions.

## 2. Business Operations (ERP Data)
- **Treasury Management**: Multi-sig (Taproot/MuSig2) for cold storage; sBTC/L2 for active yield generation.
- **Payroll**:
  - UBI (Universal Basic Income) integrations for automated distribution.
  - Streaming payments via Lightning (LND/Core Lightning).
- **Invoicing**: Native Bitcoin invoices (BIP-21) and Lightning invoices (BOLT-11).
- **Compliance**: ZK-proofs for "Proof of Reserves" and "Proof of Solvency" without leaking PII.

## 3. Platform Integration Points (Conxian)
- **Gateway**: Bridges traditional ERP inputs (JSON/CSV) to Bitcoin-native transactions.
- **Nexus**: Indexes corporate state (UTXOs, Merkle Roots) for verifiable audits.
- **AI Allocation**: Computing resources treated as a liquid asset/commodity for the business.

## 4. Unknowns & Evolving Realities
- **BitVM**: Complex, trustless bridging between L1 and L2.
- **RGB**: Client-side validated smart contracts for private asset issuance.
- **Sovereign Regulation**: The shift from centralized exchange reporting to self-hosted node audits.
