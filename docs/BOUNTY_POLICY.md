# Bounty Policy

Bounties exist to reward genuine, net-new, code-scoped contributions. To protect contributor trust and verifiability, the following rules apply to every bounty in this repository:

1. **Code-scoped only.** Bounties attach only to concrete, code-completable issues. Internal research tickets, strategic/legal decisions, and human-gated governance items are never bountiable.
2. **Written acceptance criteria required before funding.** A bounty is not "open" until the issue states explicit, testable acceptance criteria.
3. **First valid submission wins; duplicates are not paid.** If the work is already completed or merged (by maintainers or a prior PR), duplicate submissions receive no payout.
4. **No payout without merge.** Payment requires a merged, accepted PR that satisfies the acceptance criteria and passes CI/review.
5. **Void on human-gate.** A bounty on an issue later found blocked on governance/legal (not code) is void, and that determination is recorded publicly on the issue.

Any exception requires documented approval from protocol governance + legal.
